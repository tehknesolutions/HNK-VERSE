# DISCOVERY 003 — PLAYER IDENTITY & PROGRESSION LOCK

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Authority:** Creator-approved HNK-VERSE product canon  
**Depends on:** DISCOVERY 002 — Product Fantasy Lock

## 1. Identity model

HNK-VERSE must not collapse human, identity, Personal Verse and avatar into one object.

`HUMAN → HNK_IDENTITY → PERSONAL_VERSE → AVATAR(S)`

### HUMAN
The real person using the product.

### HNK_IDENTITY
Persistent account-level identity and provenance layer. It owns permissions, achievements, authoritative runtime history and Verse links.

### PERSONAL_VERSE
Each HNK Identity has **one primary Personal Verse**. This is the user's persistent universe and main manifestation container.

A Personal Verse may contain multiple worlds, timelines, lands, homes, organizations, Agents, creatures and playable embodiments without fragmenting identity continuity.

### AVATAR
A playable embodied representation inside a world/context.

**Multiple avatars are allowed inside the same Personal Verse.**

An avatar is not the human, not the HNK Identity and not the Personal Verse itself.

## 2. Progression means expanded capability

Traditional level values may exist, but core progression is the expansion of what the player can actually perceive, understand, practice, make and govern.

`PERCEIVE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → MAKE → TRANSFORM → ORGANIZE → GOVERN → DEFINE RULES → CREATE WORLDS`

The final fantasy is not merely a stronger avatar. It is increased capacity to manifest structured change.

## 4. Sephirotic meta-architecture

The Tree of Life remains a **universal deep meta-architecture of HNK-VERSE**.

User-created worlds may expose different surface progression systems, cultures, aesthetics, technologies, magic systems and local rules, but they remain nested inside HNK-VERSE's deeper HNK architecture.

Therefore:

`LOCAL WORLD PROGRESSION ≠ HNK META-ARCHITECTURE`

A world does not need to look explicitly Kabbalistic to remain connected to the HNK structure.

## 5. Parallel progression dimensions

The player may progress independently but interactively across:

- **Knowledge** — learned, verified and unlocked knowledge.
- **Language** — what can be named, read and expressed through HNK-Idioma.
- **Skill** — repeatable executable capability.
- **Profession** — coherent mastery cluster.
- **Relationship** — trust/history with humans, Agents and creatures.
- **Creation** — objects and structures that can be produced.
- **Territory** — scale of world that can be modified.
- **Civilization** — scale of systems that can be organized.
- **World Authority** — local rules the user may configure.
- **Manifestation** — ability to turn knowledge, intention and action into persistent world state.

## 6. Three independent authority axes

### Runtime authority
What the system technically permits a user/entity to do.

### World authority
What a specific world and its diegetic rules permit an avatar/entity to do.

### Canonical authority
What may define or modify HNK canon.

These are never silently equivalent.

A World Creator may possess enormous local world authority without gaining authority to redefine HNK canon.

## 7. World Creator freedom

World Creators may create worlds that diverge strongly from HNK defaults at the local/world layer.

Configurable domains may eventually include:

- gravity and movement;
- climate and time;
- resource rules;
- economy;
- PvP and conflict;
- magic/technology availability;
- creatures and ecology;
- construction permissions;
- death/respawn rules;
- Agent behavior constraints;
- language usage;
- progression systems;
- visual and cultural rules.

However local rule freedom cannot silently override:

- HNK canonical authority;
- provenance;
- security boundaries;
- Lucidity invariants;
- platform-level safety/governance;
- Personal Verse identity continuity.

## 8. Persistent Agent minimum

A persistent Agent is not merely a scripted NPC if, at minimum, it possesses:

`STABLE_ID + MEMORY + HISTORY + RELATIONSHIPS + GOALS + WORLD_STATE + CONTINUITY`

Additional capabilities may include:

- skills;
- home;
- inventory;
- occupation;
- knowledge;
- plans;
- bounded permissions;
- personality state;
- social affiliations.

Agent continuity may generate **diegetic world history**.

Agent speech, belief or interpretation is never automatically HNK canon, evidence or scientific fact.

## 9. Creature relationship

Creatures are not defined only as combat units.

Valid systemic roles include:

- companions;
- ecology participants;
- exploration partners;
- resource finders;
- workers;
- transport;
- producers;
- guardians;
- training/bond partners.

Any canonical relation between HNK-VERSE creatures and other HNK universes remains a separate future Creator decision.

## 10. Fundamental player loops

Personal capability loop:

`DISCOVER → LEARN → NAME → PRACTICE → GATHER → CREATE → USE → TRANSFORM → OBSERVE → RECORD → PROGRESS`

Civilization-scale loop:

`BUILD → CONNECT → ORGANIZE → GOVERN → DEFINE → CREATE WORLD`

## 11. ZERO identity proof

ZERO must prove only:

1. one HNK Identity persists;
2. one primary Personal Verse persists;
3. one playable avatar exists;
4. one land/home belongs to that Personal Verse;
5. the avatar can learn one thing;
6. learning unlocks one executable capability;
7. that capability creates one persistent world change;
8. one Agent preserves one meaningful interaction;
9. telemetry records provenance;
10. logout/login preserves the result.

Support for multiple avatars, multiple worlds and advanced world authority is architectural, not required for ZERO UI.

## 12. Locked decisions

1. **One HNK Identity has one primary Personal Verse.**
2. **A Personal Verse may contain multiple playable avatars.**
3. **The Sephirotic Tree is the universal deep meta-architecture; worlds may have their own surface progression.**
4. **World Creators may define radically different local rule systems while remaining inside HNK-VERSE governance and canon boundaries.**
5. **Persistent Agents require stable identity, memory, history, relationships, goals, world state and continuity at minimum.**
