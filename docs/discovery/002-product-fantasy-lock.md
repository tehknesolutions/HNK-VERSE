## 1. Core product statement

HNK-VERSE is the executable and experiential manifestation of the HNK ecosystem: a persistent personal universe in which the user lives, learns, expresses, creates, explores and transforms worlds while events, symbols, language, knowledge, Agents and actions maintain structured relationships with HNK metaphysical cosmology.

Gameplay genre shorthand:

**Persistent Life-Sim RPG Social Creation Sandbox + Civilization + AI Agents + Multiverse**

This shorthand describes gameplay. It does not replace the ontological product definition above.

## 2. HNK Core Triad

- **CODEX-HNK — SABER**
- **HNK-IDIOMA — EXPRESSAR**
- **HNK-VERSE — MANIFESTAR**

The three cores form a loop rather than isolated products:

`CODEX → LANGUAGE → INTENTION → ACTION → MANIFESTATION → WORLD CHANGE → EXPERIENCE → RECORD → KNOWLEDGE → CODEX`

## 3. Project-canonical ontology

Within HNK project canon, HNK precedes HNK-VERSE.

HNK is treated by the project as the astral-mental dimension described by its Creator from prior personal practice and experience. CODEX-HNK, HNK-IDIOMA and HNK-VERSE are formalizations/manifestations of that pre-existing HNK framework.

Therefore HNK-VERSE does not define HNK as a fictional dimension invented by the software. HNK-VERSE is an executable interface and world-system intended to express, experience and manifest the HNK framework.

This is a project-canonical metaphysical proposition. Runtime and documentation must not silently reclassify metaphysical interpretation as scientific evidence.

## 4. Player fantasy

> I enter HNK-VERSE as an individual with a small place in the world. I live, learn, explore and discover how reality works. What I learn becomes capability. Capability lets me perceive resources, master skills, build tools, transform matter, form relationships, create a home and alter my environment. I may join or found groups, settlements, cities and civilizations; explore dungeons, territories and worlds; interact with persistent Agents and creatures; and eventually progress from living under the rules of worlds to creating systems, worlds and a Verse of my own.

## 5. Personal Verse

The primary user container is not merely an account or character slot.

`HUMAN → IDENTITY → PERSONAL_VERSE → WORLD(S) → LAND → HOME → AVATAR`

A **Personal Verse** is the persistent universe associated with a user identity. It may contain:

- world history;
- lands and structures;
- player-created objects;
- persistent Agents;
- creatures;
- relationships;
- organizations;
- discoveries;
- language state;
- CODEX knowledge state;
- practices and records;
- skills and professions;
- economy and resources;
- world rules;
- portals and connections;
- provenance and telemetry.

Future multiplayer is therefore allowed to be modeled as intersections between Verses, not only as multiple avatars sharing one server map.

## 6. Event ontology

A meaningful HNK-VERSE event may carry multiple simultaneous layers:

1. **Executable Event** — what verifiably happened in runtime.
2. **Narrative Event** — what it means in the diegetic world/history.
3. **HNK Correspondence** — its defined symbolic/metaphysical relation inside HNK canon.
4. **Experience Record** — what the user reports observing/experiencing.
5. **Interpretation** — meaning assigned by user or Agent.
6. **Evidence** — support that can actually be demonstrated.
7. **Canon** — approved/versioned authoritative HNK content.

These layers must remain distinguishable in data and UI.

## 7. Lucidity invariant

`EXPERIENCE ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ EVIDENCE ≠ CANON`

Extended runtime form:

`ACTION ≠ OBSERVATION ≠ RESULT ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ EVIDENCE ≠ CANON`

Metaphysical correspondence may be represented faithfully without allowing coincidence, Agent output or player interpretation to auto-promote itself into evidence or HNK canon.

## 8. World layers

HNK-VERSE may expose multiple interacting layers of one experience:

- **Physical Layer** — movement, objects, construction, resources, economy.
- **Mental Layer** — memory, knowledge, dreams, interpretation, learning.
- **Symbolic Layer** — language, glyphs, names, correspondences, rituals.
- **Metaphysical Layer** — relationships defined by HNK cosmology.
- **Social Layer** — humans, groups, institutions and Verse intersections.
- **Creator Layer** — rules, systems, structures, worlds and Verse creation.

The beginner need not see all layers at once. Progressive revelation is part of progression.
